
##########################
 Better Unclothed Texture
for Mount & Blade: Warband
##########################

M&B:W rewards the player for arena fights early game and sparring training mid/late game.
In the practice arena and in sparring training you are always unclothed, no armour.
I got tired of the boring brown rags and decided to make something slightly better.


#############
Compatibility
#############

Works perfectly in Mount & Blade: Warband vanilla.

Since it's just a simple texture replacement, this shouldn't conflict with other mods or versions (untested).


############
Instructions
############

1. Find your Mount & Blade: Warband folder, where your game is installed.

2. You should see two subfolders called "Textures" and "TexturesHD".

3. (Optional) from those folders, create backup copies of these texture files that will be replaced.
   If you prefer, you can re-verify files on Steam to restore the originals if desired:

-- man_body.dds (From "TexturesHD" folder)
-- man_body8.dds (From "Textures" folder)
-- body_normalmap.dds  (From both "Textures" and "TexturesHD" folders)

4. Unzip all the .dds files from the download into the "Textures" folder, and again into the "TexturesHD" folder.
